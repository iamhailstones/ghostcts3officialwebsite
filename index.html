01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE SCREEN

Who is Hailstones?

I'm a developer and creative mind who enjoys taking an idea and making something real out of it. I work across front-end and back-end development, explore how software works, and build projects that give me room to experiment.

I'm also a game creator. I like the mix of design, logic, action and storytelling that makes a game feel like more than just code on a screen. My current project, GHOST CTS-3, brings that mindset together with something else I enjoy: Python.

I don't claim to know everything. I'm learning as I build, testing ideas, breaking things, fixing them, and sharing the journey. I want my work to show curiosity, effort and a willingness to try something different.

Outside the code, I'm a guy who loves life and art, enjoys connecting with people, and believes you can be serious about building something without losing your personality.

02 / CURRENTLY BUILDING

Projects from the shadows.

FEATURED PROJECT

GHOST CTS-3

CODE TO SURVIVE

GHOST CTS-3 is a browser-based action shooter built around a simple idea: make learning Python feel more interactive. Instead of keeping programming concepts inside a textbook, the game connects them to the mechanics of surviving a fight.

It combines a soldier-style action experience with Python-inspired systems. The project is evolving as I test it, improve the gameplay, and explore how to make the coding ideas clearer and more fun.
